Humans, agents, observers, and tools are all `Participant`s of the same `AgenticEnvironment`. Each participant runs **non-blocking** and streams typed `ContextItem`s into the environment. Every other participant sees those items in real time and can react, intercept, or stay silent — without any central scheduler.

## Why Mozaik

- **Non-blocking participants** — Capability methods return `Promise<void>` and stream items into the environment as they are produced. No participant ever blocks another, even on a slow model call or a long-running tool.
- **Shared agentic environment** — `AgenticEnvironment` is a broadcast bus, not a pipeline. There is no central scheduler deciding who runs next; participants `join()` and react to whatever flows through.
- **Reaction over orchestration** — A single `onContextItem(source, item)` intercept lets a participant observe, react, or stay silent. Add a critic, a logger, or a guardrail without touching existing participants.
- **Composable generators** — Swap `InputItemSource`, `InferenceRunner`, and `FunctionCallRunner` to change how input is produced, how the model is called, and how tools are resolved — without changing the participant contract.
- **OpenResponses-aligned types** — `ContextItem` mirrors the [OpenResponses](https://www.openresponses.org/) vocabulary, so your domain types stay stable across providers.
